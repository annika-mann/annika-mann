## 🧭 How I Approach Product Work

- Build shared understanding before building software.
- Prototype to learn, not to convince.
- Understand people before optimizing solutions.
- Design systems, not isolated features.
- Look beyond the interface to the teams, processes, handovers and dependencies behind it.
- Make decisions visible, test assumptions, and keep improving.

## 📖 How to Read These Repositories

The portfolio is not intended as a frontend engineering showcase. Its purpose is to make product thinking, workflows, decisions and trade-offs tangible.

Depending on the case, you may find unbranded explorations, design-system or MVP reconstructions, and future-oriented product visions. Each stage answers a different question; not every case needs every stage.

The repositories and case studies provide the detailed context. For the **five areas I'm focusing on next**, see my [Portfolio Backlog & Roadmap](PORTFOLIO-BACKLOG.md).
